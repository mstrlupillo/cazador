# Profile template

Write the user's profile in this shape. Keep it under ~250 words, factual, and in the third
person ("he/she/they" as the user prefers). Every line must come from their CV or their answers.

```
WHO: <Name> — <current role / program, graduation or availability date>. ~<N> years of experience:
<role at company (one-line what they did)>; <role at company>; <role at company>.
Languages: <languages>. Can work without sponsorship in: <countries>.
DIFFERENTIATORS: <3–5 things that make them stand out, e.g. "AI go-to-market", "built partnerships
with global consumer brands", "founder", "lived in 8 countries">.
TARGET ROLES, in priority order: <role family 1>; <role family 2>; …
COMPANIES: <types/industries they want, e.g. "tech (AI, SaaS, fintech), growth stage to big tech">.
SENIORITY: <titles that fit, e.g. "Manager, Senior Manager, Lead; Head of at startups">.
HARD NO: <excluded functions, industries, companies>; internships and roles requiring a language
they don't speak.
CITIES: <city 1>, <city 2>, …
PAY CURRENCY: <USD by default>
```
